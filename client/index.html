We built Nexus to replace the patchwork of tools most SMBs cobble together — spreadsheets, disconnected SaaS apps, and manual data entry.

Multi-Tenant & Team Ready

Manage multiple organizations under one account with role-based access control and granular permissions per team.

Real-Time Inventory

Track stock levels, warehouse transfers, and purchase orders in real time — no manual reconciliation required.

Built-In Accounting

A full double-entry ledger, automated invoicing, and expense tracking — no separate accounting software needed.

Tenant-scoped data access and role-based permissions help keep each organization's records separate and accessible to the right people.
